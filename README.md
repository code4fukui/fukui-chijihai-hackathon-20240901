# fukui-chijihai-hackathon-20240901

> 日本語のREADMEはこちらです: [README.ja.md](README.ja.md)

This repository is for the **Fukui Governor's Cup Hackathon 2024**, a civic tech event where participants collaborate with local governments to propose and develop open-source solutions for regional challenges in Fukui Prefecture, Japan.

## Event Report

A report from Day 1 (September 28, 2024) is available, showcasing the city challenge presentations, team ideation sessions, and the beginner's coding workshop.

- **[View the Day 1 Report](report/index.html)**

## Challenges for 2024

This year's hackathon focuses on challenges presented by two municipalities:

-   **Tsuruga City: Promoting the Use of Vacant Houses**
    -   **Goal:** Encourage owners to sell or rent vacant properties and create a system for people who want to utilize them. The existing "Vacant House Information Bank" has low registration, and the city is seeking new solutions.

-   **Sabae City: Digital Platform for Citizen Engagement**
    -   **Goal:** Develop a digital platform to better reflect citizens' voices in policy-making. The aim is to foster a culture where citizens can proactively propose and implement solutions to social issues.

## Awards

-   **Fukui Governor's Cup (Grand Prize):** ¥100,000
-   **Commentator's Awards (Special Prizes):** ¥50,000 for two teams

## Features

-   Direct collaboration with city officials from Tsuruga and Sabae.
-   Solutions are developed as open-source software for implementation by municipalities.
-   Technical mentoring provided throughout the development period.
-   A hands-on **Beginner's Workshop** on web app development and civic tech is held on the morning of Day 1.

## How to Participate

1.  Review the [application guidelines](https://code4fukui.github.io/fukui-chijihai-hackathon-20240901/appreq.html) (in Japanese).
2.  Submit your application through the [official form](https://code4fukui.github.io/fukui-chijihai-hackathon-20240901/) by the deadline.
3.  Attend the events, collaborate with your team, and build a solution.

Participation is open to individuals and teams. All developed solutions must be provided to Fukui Prefecture as open-source software.
